a Take starts one query for that exact Project/revision/ref, outside recording
and mutation tails. Do not hash every saved recording just because Perform
mounted or a list refreshed. No-WAV selection performs no metadata RPC. No
unbounded cache or independent timer/poll/retry loop is introduced. SDK/Native
still retain their actual unfinished-worker debt after cancellation.

The list is bound to the Project owner that requested it and latest refresh
generation. Late lists/results cannot be adopted after close/reopen, selection,
revision or Artifact changes. The view hides an old owner's facts immediately,
before effects start its replacement query. Typed errors are rendered as safe
distinct states, and an explicit retry can recheck the currently selected WAV.
Starting a controller Project mutation also retires the selected metadata
observer without waiting for its full-WAV read to finish.
The list refresh uses two existing bounded header queries (Performances and
recovery candidates). Publish neither partial snapshot as a saved-only list;
failed recovery observation leaves list verification unavailable with explicit
retry. This does not add a full-audio scan, alter recovery mutations or replace

recovery drafts stay outside Saved Takes until their original owners settle.
These recovery-capable header reads and managed WAV install/bind exclude each
other in both directions. Retain every in-flight header peer until terminal,
even when another peer rejects. Reads remain concurrent and generation guarded;
ordinary Project edits and full WAV metadata do not wait for these header reads.
After a failed bind, refuse further recovery-capable list I/O until explicit
retry binds the original WAV or Discard commits. A committed discard releases
this restriction before its final list projection. Do not change writer retry,
Save UNKNOWN or current revision ownership.

Verified frames/rate is formatted as mm:ss.mmm using the same frame-derived
formatter as the current recording card. Unsupported WAV keeps its verified
identity but no invented frames. Corruption never presents a successful time.
Complete or prefix knowledge of the current local recording stays in the
