  capture clocks become unavailable; actual local prefix receipts remain scoped
  to their retained take. Controller close clears visible progress.

  the existing sink callbacks, actual durable changes after append/checkpoint,
  known drops after their owning updates, and final settled=true only after
  the original seal receipt. It creates no timer, RPC, I/O, stop or retry.
  Missing telemetry never changes recording admission or ownership.

  counts, absent callbacks do not. Writer durableFrames and its actual format
  determine local WAV audio. A pending transaction is not durable. Queue/WAV
  known drops and SDK unknown loss remain distinct, never summed twice.
- Keep immutable snapshots through Stop/failed prefixes/Save UNKNOWN/retry-bind.
  Clear on confirmed idle/finalization, new recording or controller close.
  Preserve original Core/capture/writer shutdown debts and all recovery paths.
