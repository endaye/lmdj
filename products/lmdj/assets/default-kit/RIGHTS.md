# LMDJ Starter Drums provenance and rights

The sixteen sounds are original percussion synthesized by
`tools/asset-server/kit.py`. Inputs are oscillator frequencies, envelopes and a
deterministic integer noise generator. There are no recorded samples, external
sample libraries, voice recordings or copied fixture artifacts.

The project owner approved an original default kit with an explicit rights
manifest in the P1 plan. LMDJ dedicates the generated audio to the public domain
under CC0 1.0 Universal. Attribution is not required. The Sound Set manifest
records `CC0-1.0`, rights holder `LMDJ` and the original synthesis provenance.

Canonical manifests, audio byte hashes and lengths are regenerated and checked
by the asset tooling. Automated format checks establish playable PCM content;
human listening and musical-quality acceptance remain separate checks.
